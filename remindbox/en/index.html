RemindBox — effortless reminders

Just take a photo and never forget. Snap bills, medicine, trash days, warranties, shopping and more — RemindBox figures out what it is and when, and reminds you on the day.

FAQ

Q. What happens to my items when I get a new iPhone?
Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and install RemindBox again. Your items, including photos, come back (RemindBox must be turned on under Settings → iCloud).

Q. The text is hard to read.
Choose a larger text size and “Easy-read mode” under Settings → Readability.

Q. How many items can I keep for free?
Up to 20 items that are not done yet. Done or deleted items don’t count. A one-time purchase removes ads and the limit (Family Sharing supported).

Q. Are my photos sent anywhere?
No. Text is read on your iPhone only. See the Privacy Policy.
