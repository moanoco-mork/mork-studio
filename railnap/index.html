Railnap — Missed Stop Alarm

Railnap wakes you up before your stop on commuter trains. It plays the alarm only through your earphones, so you can doze off without bothering anyone around you.

How it works

Choose the station where you get on and the station where you get off, then tap Start ride. Railnap watches your location during the ride and rings about 0.6 miles before your stop. It also keeps a timer based on the train schedule, so it still wakes you when GPS is weak — erring on the early side, never late.

It works offline. Station and schedule data are built into the app.

Supported railroads

Caltrain, MBTA Commuter Rail, Metra, SEPTA Regional Rail, Long Island Rail Road, Metro-North Railroad, and Metrolink. More railroads will be added in future updates.

Tips

  • Use earphones. Without earphones the alarm vibrates only, so it doesn't ring out loud on a quiet train.
  • Allow location access "While Using the App" so Railnap can follow your ride while the screen is locked.
  • When you get off, tap I got off. Railnap remembers your actual travel time and becomes more accurate next time.

Schedule data

Schedule data: Caltrain GTFS; MassDOT (MBTA Commuter Rail GTFS); Metra GTFS; SEPTA Regional Rail GTFS; MTA (Long Island Rail Road and Metro-North Railroad GTFS); Metrolink GTFS. The data was obtained from these agencies and is redistributed within the app. Railnap is not sponsored by, affiliated with, or operated by any of these agencies. Schedules may be inaccurate or out of date; always check official information.
