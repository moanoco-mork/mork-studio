Railnap — open station data

Railnap bundles station data derived from open data. Data derived from sources licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) is published here under the same licence.

France — SNCF

  • File: sncf-stations.json (stations, coordinates, adjacent stations and typical travel times derived from the SNCF Voyageurs GTFS).
  • Source: SNCF Voyageurs open data (GTFS), licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
  • This derived database is made available under the ODbL 1.0. Any rights in individual contents are licensed under the Database Contents License.
  • Format: {"source", "lines": [[operator, line]], "stations": [[id, name, line, group, lat, lon, [[neighbour id, metres, seconds], ...]]], "fastest": {"a-b": seconds}}

Railnap is not sponsored by, affiliated with, or operated by SNCF.
