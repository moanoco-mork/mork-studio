Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the app "Railnap" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect any information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or location. Nothing is sent to our servers.

2. Location

The App uses your iPhone's location only while an alarm is running, to know when your stop is near. It also uses your location to suggest the nearest station when you choose where to get on.

Your location is used only on your iPhone. It is never sent or stored anywhere else. When the alarm ends, the App stops using your location.

3. Update check

When you open the App, it may read a small file from our website (mork-studio.net) to check whether a newer version is available. No personal information or location is sent. As with any website request, the hosting provider may log technical data such as your IP address.

4. Data stored on your iPhone

Your favorite routes, your actual travel times, your alarm settings, and the number of free rides you have used are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the App deletes this data (except the count of free rides used).

5. In-app purchase

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information.

6. Ads, analytics, and third parties

The App shows no ads and uses no analytics tools. We do not share your information with third parties.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
